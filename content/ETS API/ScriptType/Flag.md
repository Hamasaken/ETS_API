# Flag : [[Any]]

A Flag is a simple on/off switch stored on a [[Character]], used for yes/no states such as whether they are Alive. Scripts rarely handle a Flag directly; instead they use the character's own [[Character#^is-535570564\|Is()]] and [[Character#^setflag-142902377\|SetFlag()]] to read and change flags by their [[FlagType]].
