# Attack : [[Any]]

An Attack describes how a strike is delivered — its kind (such as Melee, Ranged or Magical) and its base accuracy. When a character attacks, this accuracy is combined with the attacker's and defender's stats to decide whether it lands. An Attack on its own does not deal any damage; that is handled separately by a [[Damage]].
